About Tax Slabs

Tax Slabs explains the income tax rates for individuals in India in plain language and lets you work out the tax under both the new and the old regime. It covers tax year 2026-27 (income earned from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027) and financial year 2025-26, which use the same rates.

Where the figures come from

  • Slab rates of both regimes, surcharge and cess for tax year 2026-27: the Memorandum explaining the provisions of the Finance Bill, 2026, published by the Ministry of Finance with the Union Budget on 1 February 2026 (indiabudget.gov.in). It states that no change is proposed in these rates.
  • The same rates, the rebate and the limits of common deductions for financial year 2025-26 (assessment year 2026-27): the help page "Salaried Individuals for AY 2026-27" on the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal (incometax.gov.in).
  • Standard deduction and the relief just above ₹12 lakh: as provided by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 and the Finance Act, 2025.

The figures were checked on 11 November 2026. The calculator and every worked example on the site are produced from one set of rules, so they always agree with each other.

What this site is not

It is not a government site and it does not give tax advice. It handles the ordinary case: a resident individual with salary, pension, interest or business income. It does not handle capital gains and other income taxed at special rates, non-residents, or relief for arrears. Tax rules change with each Budget. Before you file a return, choose a regime or make an investment for tax reasons, check with the Income Tax Department or a qualified professional.
