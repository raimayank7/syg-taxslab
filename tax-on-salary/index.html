Reading the table

  • Salary is the salary for the whole year before any deduction.
  • New regime tax takes the standard deduction of ₹75,000 and the rebate into account. It is nil up to a salary of ₹12,75,000.
  • Share of salary is the tax divided by the salary. It stays far below the top slab rate of 30%, because only the top slice of income is taxed at the top rate.
  • Old regime tax here assumes only the standard deduction of ₹50,000. With 80C, health insurance, home loan interest or house rent allowance it would be lower: use the calculator.
  • Above a salary of about ₹50 lakh a surcharge is added, which is why the tax rises faster there.

The "tax a month" figure is the yearly tax divided by 12. Employers deduct tax from salary in a similar way, spread over the months left in the year.
