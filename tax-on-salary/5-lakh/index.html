Income tax on a salary of ₹5 lakh, tax year 2026-27

On a salary of ₹5,00,000 (₹5 lakh) in tax year 2026-27, a resident salaried person pays no income tax in the new regime. In the old regime with no deductions other than the standard deduction, the tax would be nil.

New regime: the working, step by step

  1. Salary for the year: ₹5,00,000
  2. Less standard deduction: ₹75,000
  3. Taxable income: ₹4,25,000

Tax on the slabs:

  1. Nil on ₹4,00,000 (the first ₹4,00,000): ₹0
  2. 5% on ₹25,000 (from ₹4,00,000 to ₹4,25,000): ₹1,250

Tax on the slabs comes to ₹1,250. The taxable income is not more than ₹12,00,000, so the rebate takes off ₹1,250 and the tax becomes ₹0. With no tax there is no cess either.

Tax for the year: nil.

After this tax, ₹5,00,000 of the salary is left for the year, about ₹41,667 a month. Provident fund, professional tax and other deductions your employer makes are separate and are not counted here.

Old regime with no deductions

  1. Salary for the year: ₹5,00,000
  2. Less standard deduction: ₹50,000
  3. Taxable income: ₹4,50,000

Tax on the slabs:

  1. Nil on ₹2,50,000 (the first ₹2,50,000): ₹0
  2. 5% on ₹2,00,000 (from ₹2,50,000 to ₹4,50,000): ₹10,000

Tax on the slabs comes to ₹10,000. The taxable income is not more than ₹5,00,000, so the rebate takes off ₹10,000 and the tax becomes ₹0. With no tax there is no cess either.

Tax for the year: nil.

Which regime costs less at ₹5 lakh?

At this salary the old regime is not costlier even without deductions, because both regimes give a nil or equal tax. There is nothing to gain or lose by choosing either.

How to compare the two regimes

If the salary rises by ₹1 lakh

At a salary of ₹6,00,000 the new regime tax is nil. That is no more tax on the extra ₹1,00,000, so ₹1,00,000 of the raise stays with you. Only the top slice of income is taxed at the higher rate; the tax on the rest does not change.

Senior citizens

The new regime has the same slabs for every age, so the figure above applies to pensioners too. In the old regime with no deductions, a person aged 60 to 79 would pay no tax and a person aged 80 or more no tax on a pension of this size.

Common questions

How much income tax on a salary of ₹5 lakh?

For tax year 2026-27 the new regime tax on a yearly salary of ₹5,00,000 is nil including cess. The old regime tax with only the standard deduction is nil.

Which regime is better for a ₹5 lakh salary?

At this salary the old regime is not costlier even without deductions, because both regimes give a nil or equal tax. There is nothing to gain or lose by choosing either.

How much tax is deducted each month?

If the employer spreads the tax evenly over twelve months, the new regime deduction on this salary is about ₹0 a month. In practice the employer recalculates it during the year when pay or declarations change.
