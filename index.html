Five facts for 2026-27

  • The new regime is the default. Its slabs are the same for every age.
  • A resident with taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 pays no tax in the new regime; for a salaried person that means a salary up to ₹12,75,000.
  • The standard deduction is ₹75,000 in the new regime and ₹50,000 in the old.
  • 4% health and education cess is added to the tax in both regimes.
  • The rates are unchanged from financial year 2025-26.

Common questions

What are the income tax slabs for FY 2026-27?

New regime: nil up to ₹4 lakh, 5% to ₹8 lakh, 10% to ₹12 lakh, 15% to ₹16 lakh, 20% to ₹20 lakh, 25% to ₹24 lakh and 30% above. Old regime below 60: nil up to ₹2.5 lakh, 5% to ₹5 lakh, 20% to ₹10 lakh and 30% above.

What is a "tax year"?

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Act of 1961. It uses one term, the tax year, for the year in which income is earned: tax year 2026-27 runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Earlier, that year was called financial year 2026-27 and its return belonged to assessment year 2027-28.

How much tax on a salary of ₹15 lakh?

In the new regime for tax year 2026-27, a salaried person pays ₹97,500 for the year on a salary of ₹15 lakh, about ₹8,125 a month.

Is the result exact?

It is exact arithmetic on the published slabs for an ordinary case. It does not cover capital gains, non-residents and other special cases. Use it to understand your tax, and confirm with the Income Tax Department or a professional before filing.
